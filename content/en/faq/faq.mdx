## What Is Brushland?

A cozy fairytale kingdom-builder from Transhuman Design where you paint the land — mountains, rivers, waterfalls and roads — and the kingdom builds itself: villagers move in automatically and houses change appearance depending on their surroundings. It was revealed October 16, 2026 with official trailers at The MIX Fall Game Showcase. The complete guide covers the whole story; the short version is that the brush is the entire interface and the kingdom is the consequence.

## Who Made Brushland?

Transhuman Design, a veteran independent studio — the developer Michał introduced the game personally on the Steam page. The studio's systems-engineering pedigree (multiplayer and creative indie titles) is the quiet reason a painting game can promise adaptive geography, houses that read surroundings, and settlers that choose their own homes.

## How Does the Painting Actually Work?

Terrain is the interface: stroke landforms and water onto the world and the game grows them into living geography — mountains swell along ridge strokes, rivers fill drawn valleys and pour off painted cliffs as waterfalls, roads wind through finished terrain. The paint mechanics guide covers every demonstrated brush and the open questions; the painting system page covers the model underneath.
